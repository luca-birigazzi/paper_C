Bounds on a weighted sum from the mean and variance, for four units

With four units the mean-zero hyperplane H is three-dimensional, and this figure is drawn inside it. The populations with mean μ and variance σ² form the sphere Sσ of radius 2σ. Populations with the same w′y lie on a circle of latitude around PHw, and the bounds are the two poles ±z*. Tick “Require non-negative values” to add the tetrahedron of non-negative populations, whose corners are the populations in which one unit holds everything.
